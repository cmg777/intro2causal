# Japanese full chapter mind maps

All six optimized WebPs in this directory are Japanese translations of the full
English chapter maps displayed on `podcasts.html`. They use the larger,
regular-width Gothic-style lettering and white-on-navy contrast approved in
Chapter 1 version 2. The six chapter cards and Chapter 1 hero on
`podcasts-ja.html` use these images, with landscape proportions and full-size
PNG links. They do not use the compact Spotify covers.

Native PNGs, exact generation prompts, source hashes, dimensions and manual
content/diagram reviews are in `mindmaps/ja/`. The selected six versions are
listed in `mindmaps/ja/manifest.json`. Chapter 1 version 1 is retained as a
historical review comparison; version 2 is the approved website asset.

The built-in image generator returned 1672 × 941 for the final images, matching
the English originals. The requested 3840 × 2160 target was not delivered.
No upscaling was applied. Typography is generated Gothic-style lettering,
not an embedded or verifiable font file.

Full copy and the original diagrams were visually checked against the English
sources and their existing econometric audit. Approximate reported findings
are retained as such. Diagrams explain concepts; artificial-data examples in
Chapter 6 remain explicitly labeled. No empirical series was newly invented.
