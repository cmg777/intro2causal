### Chapter 1: Randomized Trials

Why do insured and uninsured people have different health outcomes? Explore selection bias, potential outcomes, and randomized assignment through the RAND Health Insurance Experiment and the Oregon Health Insurance Experiment. Learn why more healthcare use need not imply large average physical-health gains.
