Chapter 2 simulations and constructed variables

Chapter 2 does not load a CSV. Its six simulation variants are generated inside the notebook (seed 42): (1) baseline selection with ability, (2) randomized/private attendance with no treatment effect, (3) treatment correlated with ability, (4) family income added as a confounder, (5) randomized treatment with a positive effect, and (6) selection on ability, income, and motivation. Each uses normally distributed ability (mean 50, SD 10), Bernoulli assignment from a logistic probability, and normal earnings noise (SD $5,000); the notebook contains exact equations and draws. They are recreated in separate code cells, so each variant resets the seed. Rows are simulated students, not source data.

Notebook-created teaching variables

Analysis variables are not CSV columns. Chapter 1 creates plan indicators, outcome means, and insured/uninsured comparison groups. Chapter 3 creates binary assigned-treatment and delivered-treatment indicators from `assigned` and `delivered`. Chapter 4 creates cutoff-side and centered-age terms plus fitted values for plots. Chapter 5 creates post/treatment interactions and year indicators; `deaths_clean.csv` rows are filtered by cause/age group for specific estimates. Chapter 6 creates twin-pair differences, quarter indicators, and regression interaction terms. Exact sample and formula are shown in the linked code cell; no single descriptive profile applies to these derived variables.
